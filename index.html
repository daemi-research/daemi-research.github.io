📡 Scientific & Technical Cooperation and Data Reception

The international project DAEMI invites profiles specialists, engineers, GIS analysts, and independent researchers to cooperate.

If you possess up-to-date monitoring results, archived databases, or field studies regarding the environmental situation in the industrial regions of the Seversky Donets River basin and the Sea of Azov, you can integrate them into our global database on a commercial basis.

📋 Materials Accepted for Verification:

  • 💧 Hydrology: Information on mine water level dynamics, reservoir status, and chemical composition of regional water bodies.
  • 🌍 Geology and Soils: Land subsidence maps over mine workings, data on soil erosion, and technogenic land contamination.
  • 💨 Atmosphere: Results of local air quality measurements (PM2.5/PM10, sulfur dioxide, nitrogen oxides).
  • 🗄️ Archives: Digitized data sheets, reports from laboratories and research institutes from past periods.

⚙️ Submission Regulations and Processing Standards

To ensure the protection of commercial secrets and researcher confidentiality, the reception of materials is automated and follows international IT standards.

1. File Preparation

Before sending graphic materials or tables, it is recommended to use the standard procedure for clearing file metadata (file properties -> "Remove personal information"). This is a standard global practice to protect the researcher's copyrights.

2. Data Upload

To prevent server overload, we do not accept heavy attachments via email. Please upload data arrays to any cloud platforms (e.g., Mega, Wormhole, or OneDrive) and provide only the download link.

💳 Compensation Procedure & Payment Gateway

3. Verification & Payout

Each received data submission undergoes automated verification. If the scientific value of the provided material is confirmed, a fee is paid to the author from the project's contracted IT fund.

4. Cryptographic Settlements

Payments are made in the international digital format USDT (TRC-20 / BEP-20). This allows direct "buyer-to-seller" settlements without delays caused by international banking compliance. Payment is sent to your specified wallet address within 48 hours after data verification.

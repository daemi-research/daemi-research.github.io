🌿 Eco-Technopark [DAEMI]

We are a community of engineers and eco-enthusiasts. Right now, we are joining forces to create the first nature-friendly technological space in our city.

🚀 Plans & Activities

The project is at the launch stage. We plan to:

  • 🤝 Participate in the development and testing of eco-installation prototypes.
  • 🧠 Conduct open lectures and practical workshops.
  • 🌱 Support local green and environmental initiatives.

💡 For Project Authors & Startups

If you have a green technology idea, a blueprint, or a ready eco-device prototype (from smart waste sorters to air/water purification systems), we are ready to offer a testing ground, engineering expertise from our community, and support with media promotion.

🔍 What Interests Us & Who We Need

We are building this project from scratch, so like-minded people are critical to us. Right now we are looking for:

  • Engineers and Inventors — to collaborate on hardware prototypes;
  • Ecologists and Scientists — for scientific expertise and idea validation;
  • IT Specialists and 3D Modellers — to create digital eco-systems;
  • Media Activists and Content Authors — to tell the world about our project;
  • Volunteers — ready to help with our first steps.

📢 Join Us!

We need active hands and bright minds. Feel free to contact us with any questions: from partnership proposals to a simple "I want to join".
